## Call for action

### FATF-1.1 Enhanced Measures for Call-for-Action Jurisdictions

Banks should apply enhanced due diligence to customers, counterparties and transactions with a meaningful connection to any jurisdiction that an international standard-setter has placed under a call for action. Institutions should refresh geography risk ratings promptly when a listing changes, re-flag affected relationships, obtain senior management approval before establishing or continuing higher-risk ties, and scrutinise the source of funds, the purpose of activity and the ultimate beneficial owners. Where risks cannot be mitigated, banks should consider restricting or exiting the relationship.

## Increased monitoring

### FATF-2.1 Responding to Jurisdictions Under Increased Monitoring

Banks should treat a jurisdiction that an international standard-setter has placed under increased monitoring as a heightened geographic risk factor, without applying a blanket refusal of business. When a country is newly flagged, banks should promptly re-assess affected customers, products and payment corridors, record the rationale for any change in risk rating, and calibrate monitoring thresholds and review frequency in proportion to the exposure. Banks should also confirm that the published deficiencies are reflected in their own risk assessment and that staff receive timely guidance on expected escalation.
