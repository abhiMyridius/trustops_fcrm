# Internal Customer Onboarding Standard 1.8 (synthetic corpus v14)

> Synthetic internal standard for demonstration. Not the policy of any real institution.

## Section 1 KYC/CDD

### CO18-1.1 Identity verification before account opening

The bank shall collect and verify identifying information for every prospective customer before an account is opened or a product is activated. Verification shall rely on documentary or non-documentary evidence from reliable, independent sources, and the method used shall be recorded in the customer file. Where identity cannot be verified within the defined period, the bank shall restrict activity, escalate to compliance, and decline or close the relationship if doubts remain unresolved.

### CO18-1.2 Beneficial ownership of legal entity customers

The bank shall identify and verify the natural persons who ultimately own or control each legal entity customer, including those holding significant ownership and at least one individual with managerial control. Ownership structures involving trusts, nominees, bearer instruments or multiple layers shall be documented in a structure chart. Where beneficial owners cannot be established with confidence, the bank shall not onboard the entity without senior compliance approval and a written rationale.

### CO18-1.3 Customer risk rating methodology at onboarding

The bank shall assign every customer an initial risk rating at onboarding, using a documented methodology that weighs customer type, expected products and services, delivery channel, and geographic exposure. Ratings shall be reproducible and recorded with the factors that drove them. Analysts may override a calculated rating only upward without approval, and any downward override shall require documented justification and review by a second qualified reviewer.

### CO18-1.4 Enhanced due diligence for higher-risk relationships

The bank shall apply enhanced due diligence to customers rated high risk, including politically exposed persons, their close associates and family members, and customers with complex or opaque structures. Enhanced measures shall include corroborating source of wealth and source of funds, obtaining senior management approval before establishing the relationship, and applying closer ongoing monitoring. The reasons for accepting each such relationship shall be documented and retained in the customer file.

### CO18-1.5 Periodic review and event-driven refresh of CDD

The bank shall keep customer due diligence information current through scheduled reviews whose frequency reflects the customer risk rating, and through event-driven refreshes. Triggering events include a material change in ownership, unexpected transaction patterns, adverse media, or a request for a new product. Reviews shall confirm that identification data, business purpose and expected activity remain accurate, and unresolved gaps shall lead to restrictions or exit decisions.

### CO18-1.6 Use of third parties in identity verification

The bank shall perform documented due diligence on any vendor or third party that supports customer identification or verification, including digital identity providers, before reliance begins. Contracts shall define data quality standards, audit rights, record access and incident notification. The bank remains accountable for verification outcomes, shall test vendor accuracy on a sample basis at least annually, and shall suspend reliance where performance falls below the agreed thresholds.

## Section 2 PEP and BO

### CO18-2.1 PEP screening at onboarding

The bank shall screen every prospective customer, and every natural person connected to a prospective legal entity customer, against its approved politically exposed person data before an account is opened. Screening shall cover domestic, foreign and international organisation roles. Potential matches shall be reviewed by a trained analyst, who shall record the reasoning for confirming or discarding each match. No account shall be activated while a potential match remains unresolved.

### CO18-2.2 Family members and close associates of PEPs

The bank shall extend its politically exposed person controls to immediate family members and known close associates of a confirmed public official. Relationship staff shall ask onboarding questions designed to reveal such links, including shared business interests and joint ownership. Where a link is identified, the customer shall be treated as a politically exposed relationship for risk rating, and the basis for that treatment shall be documented in the customer file.

### CO18-2.3 Identifying beneficial owners of entities

The bank shall identify and verify each individual who ultimately owns a defined percentage of a legal entity customer, together with at least one individual who exercises effective control over it. The ownership percentage applied shall be set out in the bank's procedures and reviewed annually. Where no individual meets the ownership test, the bank shall rely on the control individual and record why no owner was identified.

### CO18-2.4 Layered ownership and nominee arrangements

The bank shall look through multi-layer ownership structures until the natural persons who ultimately own or control the customer are established. Structures that involve nominee shareholders, bearer instruments, trusts or holding companies in secrecy-oriented territories shall be escalated for enhanced review. Onboarding staff shall obtain a structure chart and supporting documents, and shall decline the relationship if the real controllers cannot be reliably established.

### CO18-2.5 Senior approval and refresh for PEP relationships

The bank shall require written approval from a designated senior compliance officer before onboarding or retaining any customer confirmed as a politically exposed person or as having such a beneficial owner. The approval shall record the source of wealth and source of funds. These relationships shall receive enhanced ongoing monitoring and a full file refresh at least annually, or sooner when a trigger event occurs.

## Section 3 Periodic refresh

### CO18-3.1 Risk-Tiered Refresh Cadence for Customer Records

The bank shall refresh customer due diligence records on a cycle set by the customer's assigned risk tier. Higher-risk customers shall be reviewed more often than standard-risk customers, and low-risk customers shall be reviewed on the longest permitted cycle. Each refresh shall confirm identity details, the stated purpose of the relationship, expected activity, and the current risk rating. Business owners shall record the completion date and the reviewer, and overdue reviews shall be reported to the control function monthly.

### CO18-3.2 Event-Driven Refresh Outside the Normal Cycle

The bank shall start an unscheduled refresh whenever a material event suggests that the existing customer profile may no longer be accurate. Such events include a sudden change in transaction patterns, adverse media about the customer, a change of name or address to a higher-risk location, a new product request, or an internal escalation from investigations staff. The refresh shall begin within a defined number of business days, and its outcome shall be documented and may change the customer's risk rating.

### CO18-3.3 Re-Verifying Ownership and Control of Entity Customers

For legal entity customers, the bank shall re-verify the beneficial owners and the individuals who exercise control at each scheduled refresh. Relationship managers shall obtain a current ownership statement, compare it with the records held, and investigate any unexplained change in structure, such as a new intermediate holding company or a transfer of significant shares. Where ownership cannot be traced to natural persons, the case shall be escalated to compliance before the refresh can be closed.

### CO18-3.4 Handling Customers Who Do Not Respond to Refresh

The bank shall apply a staged escalation when a customer fails to respond to a refresh request. After the first missed deadline the bank shall send a reminder through an alternate contact method. If the customer remains unresponsive, the bank shall place reasonable limits on new products and services. Continued non-response shall lead to a documented decision on restricting or exiting the relationship, and compliance shall decide whether any unusual activity report is warranted before the account is closed.

## Section 4 Segment attestations

### CO18-4.1 Business owner attestation before segment launch

The bank shall require the accountable business owner to sign a written segment attestation before any new customer segment is opened for onboarding. The attestation shall confirm that the segment's inherent financial-crime risks have been assessed, that onboarding procedures are matched to those risks, and that staffing and monitoring capacity are sufficient. No account shall be opened for the segment until the attestation is recorded and acknowledged by Compliance.

### CO18-4.2 Expected activity profile attestation

The bank shall require each segment attestation to state the expected activity profile for the segment, including typical transaction types, value ranges, volumes and counterparties. Relationship managers shall confirm that the profile reflects the actual customers being onboarded and not a generic template. Where observed activity departs materially from the attested profile, the bank shall escalate the variance for review and may require the attestation to be reissued.

### CO18-4.3 Ownership and control attestation for entities

The bank shall require that, for any segment consisting of legal entities, the attestation confirms the method used to identify owners and controllers and the way their identities are verified. The business shall state which ownership structures fall outside the segment, such as layered or bearer-share arrangements, and shall direct those applicants to enhanced due diligence instead of standard onboarding.

### CO18-4.4 Attestation renewal and material change triggers

The bank shall renew every segment attestation at least once a year and whenever a material change occurs. Material changes include a new product offered to the segment, a new feature that alters how funds move, a change in onboarding process, or expansion into a new country. An attestation that has lapsed shall suspend new onboarding into the segment until it is renewed and approved.

### CO18-4.5 Vendor-assisted onboarding attestation and challenge

The bank shall require a supplementary attestation whenever a third-party vendor performs any part of onboarding for a segment. The attestation shall describe which checks the vendor performs, how the bank tests the vendor's output, and how exceptions are returned to the bank. The second line shall independently challenge the attestation and record its conclusions before the vendor is permitted to process live applications.
