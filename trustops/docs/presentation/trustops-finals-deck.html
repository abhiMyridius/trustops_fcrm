What the bank assesses 6 change types → the FFIEC exam manual's 4 risk domains
Change a business unit raisesCategory examiners assess
The FFIEC BSA/AML manual is what US examiners use to assess AML, CFT and sanctions risk. Mapping each change to its domains is what makes two assessments comparable.
