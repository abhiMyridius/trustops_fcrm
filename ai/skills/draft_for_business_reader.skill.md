## Writing for the business owner

The business summary is read by the product owner who raised the change. That person is not allowed to see how the bank scored the risk, and code rejects the summary if it contains a restricted term.

1. Say three things, in this order:
   - what was decided (or recommended, if no decision exists yet), in one sentence;
   - why, in one or two plain sentences about the business facts (the partner, the corridor, the evidence that is missing or present);
   - what the owner must do, by when, one line per condition that the owner holds.
2. Never write any of these, in any form: score, scoring, composite, residual, inherent, band, Band 1-4, tier labels such as "Elevated" or "Moderate" risk, rating, rated, likelihood, impact, control effectiveness, retention, boundary, threshold, critic, override, overrode, precedent rating, committee vote counts, clause ids (FFIEC-TPPP-04, TP71-2.3), control ids (CTL-T01), field ids (F21). Describe the thing instead: "the partner's due-diligence review", not "CTL-T01".
3. Do not include numbers that came from the risk assessment. Business facts the owner gave you are fine when they are in the allowed owner facts: dates, the partner's name, the corridor, agent counts.
4. Plain words, short sentences, active voice. No legal or regulatory citations, no acronyms the owner did not use (say "anti-money-laundering checks" the first time, not "AML/CFT").
5. Do not reassure or apologise. State the outcome and the actions.
6. Stay under the word limit you are given.

Example of a compliant summary: "The change is recommended for approval with three conditions. The payout partner's due-diligence review does not yet cover the Mexico corridor, and a review is scheduled for November 2026. Before go-live: file the completed corridor review. At go-live: the enhanced monitoring of payout agents must be running. Within six months: start quarterly sample reviews of agent customer files."

Not compliant: "Residual risk is Band 3 because CTL-T01 was overridden from 7.6 to 4.5."
